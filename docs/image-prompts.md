# Saltopia — image briefs for Grok

One brief per place. Paste the **STYLE** block first, then one **PLACE** block, and ask
for a 16:9 image. Save each result over the render that is there today, at the path
given — the site picks it up with no code change:

- hero of a place: `web/public/images/heroes/<slug>.webp` (1600×900)
- ticket photo of an experience: `web/public/images/experiences/<slug>.webp` (1200×900)

Today every one of these files is a render of the 3D map itself (see design.md D24), so
the site is complete without Grok. The briefs below describe each place **as it is on
the map**, so an illustration matches the view the visitor just flew to.

---

## STYLE (paste before every place)

> Stylized low-poly 3D illustration, soft aerial view from the south at a low angle,
> late sunset: violet-blue zenith fading to orange and gold at the horizon, warm key
> light from the right, cool blue-grey shadows. Setting: the highlands of Serra
> Catarinense near Lages, Brazil — rolling green hills (coxilhas), araucaria pines with
> flat umbrella crowns and bare trunks, a turquoise reservoir with sparkling water, low
> golden haze in the distance. Clean geometric shapes, gentle vertex-colour gradients,
> no textures, no outlines. NOT alpine, NOT tropical, no palm-fringed beaches, no snow
> peaks. No people's faces in close-up. Absolutely no text, letters, signs, logos or
> watermarks. Aspect 16:9.

## PLACES

### 1. Praça do Pinhão — `praca-do-pinhao`
> A small lakeside town square on a turquoise reservoir: a white octagonal bandstand
> with a green roof, red and green café umbrellas, benches, a short timber pier with a
> moored white yacht, a paved path down to the water, a stone chapel with a slate spire
> just behind, and the community's street with its dashed centre line curving past.
> Araucaria pines and round olive trees around the square. Sunset light on the water.

### 2. Galpão do Fogo de Chão — `galpao-do-fogo`
> A big rustic barn-hall (galpão) with a dark timber roof and open sides, inland on a
> green slope east of a small town, smoke from a ground fire pit rising beside it, a
> dirt driveway ending in a yard with a few parked cars, split-rail fences, a woodpile,
> araucaria pines and a conifer stand behind. Warm sunset, long shadows.

### 3. Mirante da Neblina — `mirante-da-neblina`
> A wooden lookout deck with railings on top of a green hill on the far west shore of a
> turquoise reservoir, a small pad at the summit, araucarias on the slope, the whole bay
> and a wooded island seen below, thin mist lying on the water, the sun low behind
> golden hills. Wide, calm, cinematic.

### 4. Bosque das Araucárias — `bosque-das-araucarias`
> A low wooded island (peninsula) in the middle of a turquoise reservoir, dense with
> araucaria pines and round olive-green broadleaf trees, three small stilt cabins on the
> south shore with short piers and a moored sailboat, a signpost at a clearing, boats
> passing in the bay. Aerial view, sunset.

### 5. Vinícola de Altitude — `vinicola-de-altitude`
> A hillside winery on the high north-east slope above a reservoir: a pale stone
> winery house with a dark roof, neat rows of vines running down the slope, a dirt track
> arriving in a yard, araucaria pines and a conifer stand at the edge, the lake and a
> dam visible below. Golden late light.

### 6. CTG Porteira do Tropeiro — `ctg-porteira-do-tropeiro`
> A gaucho tradition hall (CTG): a long low timber building with a wide covered porch
> and a big double gate of posts ("porteira"), a gravel yard with a few cars, paddock
> fences, a flagpole, araucarias around, in the south-east of a small lakeside town.
> Warm evening light.

### 7. Estação Velha — `estacao-velha`
> A small heritage railway station with a long platform roof on timber posts and a
> red-brick station house, a short stub of old track with weeds, a market stall under the
> roof, at the edge of a small town, araucaria pines and rolling green hills behind.
> No train. Sunset.

### 8. Pousada da Geada — `pousada-da-geada`
> A three-storey timber inn (pousada) with dark-red walls, white window trims and a
> dark roof, at the south end of a turquoise lake, a small pool, a paved car park with
> six cars beside it, a lawn, a few araucaria pines and a fence, the lake glittering in
> the sunset behind.

### 9. Fazenda do Cedro — `fazenda-do-cedro`
> A cattle farm on a high plateau behind a ridge: a big red barn with a dark roof and a
> hayloft door, two grey grain silos, a white two-storey farmhouse with a dark tile
> roof, an open machine shed, hay bales, a fenced paddock, a dirt track arriving from
> the south. Rolling green coxilhas and araucarias all around, hazy golden hills far
> behind. Aerial, sunset.

### 10. Fazenda Santa Bárbara — `fazenda-santa-barbara`
> A historic farm on an open plateau: an ochre-walled barn with a dark roof, two silos,
> an old white farmhouse of 1912 with a veranda, a tropeiro's galpão, fenced paddocks
> with cattle, a dirt track to the forecourt, araucaria groves, green rolling hills
> dissolving into golden haze. Aerial, sunset.

### 11. Fazenda dos Pinheiros — `fazenda-dos-pinheiros`
> A farm among centuries-old araucaria pines on a plateau: a timber barn with a slate
> roof, two silos, a white farmhouse, a paddock, a dirt track, and a dense pinheiral of
> tall araucarias with their flat crowns catching the last sun. Hazy hills behind.
> Aerial, sunset.

### 12. Parque Caveiras — `parque-caveiras`
> A small fairground on a green slope at the edge of a lakeside town: a Ferris wheel
> with red, teal and yellow gondolas, a red-roofed carousel, a teal-roofed bumper-car
> pavilion, a red drop tower, a chair swing, food kiosks with striped awnings, a
> ticket gate with two red pylons, strings of warm bulbs, a bandstand, a paved midway,
> a split-rail fence. Araucarias behind. Evening light, lights just coming on.

### 13. Deck do Lago — `deck-do-lago`
> A lakeside restaurant: a timber hall with a dark slate roof and a wine-red awning on
> the street side, a wide wooden deck on piles reaching over turquoise water with tables
> under wine-red umbrellas, strings of bulbs, a short jetty with a moored boat, palms
> and araucarias along the shore, a town street behind. Sunset on the water.

### 14. Porto de OVNIs — `porto-de-ovnis`
> A UFO landing port on a high plateau at the far end of a valley: a round three-tier
> concrete apron with a painted red and gold target, beacon masts with orange lamps, a
> glass-domed terminal, a control tower with a glazed cab, a radar dish, an arched
> hangar, a landed silver saucer on legs with a boarding gantry, a second saucer
> hovering on a soft golden beam of light, a fence with a gate arch, an approach lane of
> lights, a windsock. Araucarias and dusk sky. Playful, not menacing.

### 15. Salto do Rio Caveiras — `salto-caveiras`
> A small stone dam across a narrow turquoise channel where a reservoir empties: water
> spilling over the crest into a white falls below, a red-brick powerhouse (usina) of
> the 1940s with tall arched windows on the south bank, a timber footbridge across the
> gorge, boulders, a lookout deck with railings, a dirt track arriving, araucarias on
> the banks, the wooded ridge behind. Sunset light on the spray.
